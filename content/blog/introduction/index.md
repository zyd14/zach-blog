## Who am I?

My name is Zach. I'm a curious software engineer who likes to build things that enable other people to build things. I'm currently a Lead Data Platform Engineer at [Empirico](https://www.empirico.com), a biotech startup developing a platform for using data, machine learning, and AI, to accelerate the development of new medicines. 

When I'm not solving problems at work I like to run, hunt, camp, cook, eat, read, and work in the garden. I currently live in Salt Lake City, Utah, having previously lived in Seattle, Portland, and Chicago. I have a wife and two wonderful kittens.

## Why am I starting this blog?

I have a few goals in mind for this blog
### Sharing experiences and giving back to the community
The interactions between all the different technologies we as software engineers use are myriad and complex. Many of us are working on conceptually very similar problems, and I think there's a lot of value in sharing experiences and learnings so we don't have to re-invent the wheel every time we pick up a technology that is new to us. 

We've all been in the situation where we're trying to figure out how to solve a particular problem, and it's hard to know where to start and time-consuming to try the multitude of different approaches that could work. Perhaps by sharing my successes and failures I can help save you some time.

### To solidify my own understanding

Language is not only important for communicating with others, but it is also critical to the way we think, understand, and internalize the world around us. Language allows us to label and categorize our experiences so we can more easily reason about them. 

By forcing myself to write about my experiences, I hope to solidify my own understanding of the concepts and ideas I'm working with, as well as highlight gaps in my understanding of the topics I'm writing about.


### To build a community
One of the biggest benefits of being active in open source communities is the people you meet. I've been fortunate to meet and learn from some incredibly smart and driven people through Github and Slack communities for various technologies. I hope that by sharing ideas and experiences in this blog I can create opportunities to meet and collaborate with more people.

## What will I write about?

The core focus of this blog will be data-oriented software engineering. I hope to include a variety of tutorials, guides, and reflections on my experiences working with different technologies. In particular I aim to explore the intersection of software engineering and big data. My current experience is primarily rooted in the Python data ecosystem (e.g. polars, pandas, pyspark, daft, etc.), scaling data-intensive applications, and building tooling to enable data engineers to be more productive. I also hope to write about other topics that I'm interested in, including running, gardening, and cooking.
